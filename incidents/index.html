Evidence strengthened

Analytics use each incident's first-reported date. Public reporting is incomplete and uneven across countries and sectors, so charts describe the monitored corpus rather than all cyber activity.

Confirmed

The affected organisation or a competent authority has acknowledged the incident.

Corroborated

At least two independent reputable sources report the incident, but official acknowledgement has not been found yet.

Reported

A reputable source reports the incident; official confirmation has not been located yet.

Claimed

A threat actor or leak site claims an incident. This is an early-warning signal, not proof.

Disputed

The affected organisation denies or directly challenges the allegation.

Evidence can change

The agent keeps source history, so a claim can move to reported, corroborated or confirmed as better evidence appears.
